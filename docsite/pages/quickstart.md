# Quickstart

The quickstart takes a clean machine to answered questions over the
[acceptance corpus](packages/neptune-platform/docs/acceptance-corpus.md): a messy hand-over of two sites
and three robot types (an AMR fleet, an arm cell and a legged patrol robot) with one incident to
reconstruct. Its steps are the README's quickstart section, included here when the site is built, so
the two never disagree.
