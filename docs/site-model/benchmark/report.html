1 · Each fix on its own

Which fix buys what. Blue is all five together.

2 · The belt-speed trade-off

CHG-311 raised CV-2 from 0.80 to 0.95 m/s on day 1. Jams rise steeply with speed and each costs 25–35 minutes under lockout. Without lens cleaning, throughput peaks near 0.85 m/s and the speed-up loses parts. With the lens cleaned every 4 h it peaks at 0.90–0.95 m/s: the speed-up was right; skipping the cleaning was the problem. Parts-out axes start at 2,500.

3 · The charge threshold and the lying gauge

amr-03's pack has aged: it reports about 22 % when it is empty, so at the site's 20 % threshold it dies on the lane.
